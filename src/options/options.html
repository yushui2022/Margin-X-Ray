Integrity rules

Why this is not another price-guessing toy
  • No fallback factory price. If the API cannot verify a range, the panel says “insufficient data” instead of inventing a number.
  • No fake precision. Factory cards show the signals, sources and dates behind the confidence label.
  • No “100% middleman” claims. The funnel counts exclusion reasons separately instead of subtracting everything from the scanned total.
  • No hidden margin math. The landed-cost waterfall is editable and always visible.
  • Demo is labeled. Sample data never pretends to be a live supplier quote.
