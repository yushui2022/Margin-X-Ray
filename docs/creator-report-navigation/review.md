## Revised header

The arrow and label share a plum-tinted return control. A dotted rule links it to a small illustrated date stamp. The date has its own serif line under the short label. At 375 px, the strip is 52 px tall, the back target is 148 × 44 px and the other shop’s price is fully visible at y=550 in an 812 px viewport. At 320 px the 52 px strip also fits one row. The date and creator name remained selectable text.

Touching the return control at 375 px and activating its visible keyboard focus at 1440 px opened the full ranking. Browser Back restored the product report. Jazzy’s hockey table and soap kept distinct source timestamps in their semantic time elements even though both were checked on Nov 19. A missing product omitted the timestamp. Opening the same video from homepage search showed its date and kept search in place.

The browser decoded the 122 × 128 px transparent WebP stamp. The static server returned HTTP 200 and a 10,066-byte image/webp response. The illustration has empty alternative text alongside the complete text date.
