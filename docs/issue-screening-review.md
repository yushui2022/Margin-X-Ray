# Product screening on creator reports

The creator report links each prespecified price record under “Screening
sample.” The product page shows the record's match decision and offers a keyboard
disclosure for search terms, primary sources, coverage and outstanding work.

On the baseline desktop and mobile product pages, “Source documents” led only
to listing and creator evidence. A reader could see the cream's price but had
no path to the recall searches. In the first revision, the per-source row count
appeared twice and the numeric result rendered too small on a phone. The final
revision consolidates the count in one 12px line and gives the Dutch site-search
result in plain English.

Author's design judgment for the screening task. Chromium checks cover all 21
product disclosures, creator-to-product links, Enter to expand and Back. The
expanded panel fits at 320, 375, 768, 1024 and 1440px. Screenshots show the
expanded Nimya cream record on [desktop](screening-product-1440.png) and
[mobile](screening-product-375.png). No accepted creator scores were available
for a live positive-finding screenshot.
