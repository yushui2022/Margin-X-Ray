Fifteen distinct creators now appear directly on the homepage in descending creator score order, with consecutive row numbers and all five original creators retained. All fifteen scores, item names and price gaps matched the versioned ranking records. For each row the linked video, caption and decoded portrait matched the archived creator entry. The original post and selected product ID matched one scored observation for that creator.

The first expanded mobile layout put the “#10” text against the adjacent avatar. Widening the small-screen ordinal column gave every two-digit row a measured gap of at least 8.6px at 320px and 375px. The final build showed all fifteen rows at 320, 375, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels without horizontal overflow or a browser error.

All fifteen receipt links opened and focused the selected product on its creator page. At phone width the last row’s receipt link showed keyboard focus and opened with Enter. The archive’s Browse all link opened the full ranking and searching a listed handle reached its creator report. The checks used Chromium on macOS with emulated viewports and saved US edition data. The published edition also passed the five viewport and source checks. The ratings reflect this internal browser review.
