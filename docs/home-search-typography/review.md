# Homepage search

| Viewport | Before | After |

The earlier search shared a desktop row with two creator links that also appeared in the archive below. Its two mode buttons required a format choice before entering a link. The updated homepage accepts creator handles, profiles, posts and Shop product URLs in one full-width field. The search heading and controls use the interface sans serif. Score methodology remains available in the ranking and receipts.

| Internal design judgment | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task access and hierarchy | 7 | 9 |
| Brand and visual composition | 8 | 9 |
| Readability and information density | 8 | 9 |
| Interaction and feedback | 8 | 9 |
| Responsive behavior and accessibility | 8 | 9 |
| Weighted mean | 7.8 | 9.0 |

On a 1440 px viewport the field grew from 526 × 56 to 1124 × 76 CSS pixels, with 20 px input text. At 375 px the field grew from 347 × 56 to 347 × 64 with 16 px input text. Chromium checked search, archive and keyboard navigation at 320, 375, 768, 1024 and 1440 px. Each view fit its viewport. The local edition also retained keyboard focus from the search shortcut and image validation at its screenshot input. The public update was checked in a fresh browser session. [Published search field](published-search.png)

## Search above the featured receipt

The search field now follows the masthead in reading and keyboard order. On a 1440 px viewport its top edge moved from 727 to 279 CSS pixels. At 375 px it moved from 613 to 226. The entire field was visible on first load at all five tested widths. Keyboard focus from the masthead, handle search and both sampled receipt links worked at each width. The public homepage was checked after deployment.

The internal placement review scored hierarchy 8 before and 9 after. Brand, readability, interaction and responsive behavior each scored 9 in both rounds. The weighted overall judgment went from 8.8 to 9.0 for this change.
