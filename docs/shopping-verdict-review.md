## Revisions checked in Chrome

The first report layout showed the result before the setup controls, but an inherited toolbar style added a redundant rule and long multi-edition product titles filled four lines on mobile. The second pass scoped the toolbar, preserved the start and end of lengthy titles, shortened the scorecard, and moved shop prices and links up the page.

The saved video check showed a $59.99 original and a $49.95 similar listing from a different shop. The report displayed a 20.1% price premium and an estimated score of 20/100. The saved creator check attributed its result to one scored product among three linked products, across five recent posts. Opening the sample disclosed all five source links. Marketplace controls and historical creator votes were outside the score computation.

Isolated responses exercised a verified $17.00 item saving, an original that cost $15.00 less, equal prices, two comparable shops with a $48.95 median, and an unscored report without products. A stored score took precedence over recomputation. Opening the evidence and methodology and changing a variant issued no new shopping job. The market selector and original video remained accessible through the report’s final disclosure. These state checks used local browser fixtures. The screenshots below use saved TikHub evidence.
