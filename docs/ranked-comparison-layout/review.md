# Ranked product price comparison

The review follows the Disney Princess air hockey table from @all_the_jazzy_things on the public US edition. The item was listed at $64.94. One other shop quoted $54.83 for a similar variant. The observed gap is $10.11 (18.4%) and the current estimated item score is 63/100.

Chromium checks used 320, 375, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels. At 1440px the two columns measured 686px and 371px with a 23px gap. The closed report and expanded evidence fit a 320px viewport. The buying-advice button opened the quote and focused its disclosure. The seller links preserved their recorded SKUs. The earlier receipt showed its own scoring version when opened. Searches using the creator handle and a recorded video link led back to saved reports. A second creator with two scored products retained both receipts.

Isolated fixtures exercised verified same-item savings, equal prices, a cheaper original offer and a similar-item median from two shops. They used the current scoring function and never wrote to the published index. The design ratings are an internal review of these browser checks. Store checkout, real-device testing and user research were outside this review.
