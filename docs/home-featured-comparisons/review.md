# Shopping homepage and creator rankings

The homepage and rankings previously opened with the same four tied creators and the same air hockey table. Fifteen featured creator rows represented four distinct TikTok Shop listing IDs. The before captures show both routes at 375 and 1440px.

The home search now leads into one receipt card per scored listing. Cards show the selected variant, creator price, comparison price and match basis. A representative observation supplies both prices and the source. Selection prefers exact matches, then broader shop coverage, then recency. The display order uses the number of distinct checked creators per product. Opening a card displays that observation’s evidence inline. Closing returns focus to the card.

## Interaction checks

- Confirmed four unique listing IDs with two prices each. All four comparisons in this edition use similar items. Each card opened the corresponding receipt and the amounts matched its evidence.
- Exercised keyboard activation and close, restoration of focus, the homepage creator search and the link to the full ranking. The published edition still has 89 ranked creators, 72 unrated creators and four tied for first place.
- Checked both languages across 320–1440px, including the single- to double-column transition. The layouts fit their viewports. Before the final pass the first two cards were both air hockey tables, and the workbench listing’s title included several unselected licensed characters. Sorting by creator coverage and shortening two verified listing titles addressed those issues. The receipt keeps the full source title.
