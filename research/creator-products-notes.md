## First directory research expansion

Selected six roster members with explicit official founder evidence. Product pages
and founder/investor announcements were checked separately. New entries are brand
associations, not evidence of a particular TikTok endorsement, post, affiliate
relationship or Shop listing. Existing public scores remain unrated.

Direct retrieval succeeded for Rare Beauty's About and mini blush pages and
PAPATUI's founder page. Ten attempted product URLs returned HTTP 429; their usable
price evidence is labeled as search-index access. Rare Beauty's full-size blush
price also appeared in the directly retrieved mini page's related-product card.
Publication dates remain null when not established. An indexed price observed on
2026-11-17 is not proof the index was refreshed that day. Source entries preserve
condensed extracts; they are not represented as archived full-page snapshots.

Feastables' official site links to MrBeast Store. Standard and King Size ten-bar
packs have distinct URLs and prices; no weight-normalized price comparison is
possible from the retained extracts. Rare Beauty full-size and mini blushes also
remain distinct, with their observed shades and missing volume details explicit.

The rhode single-item record uses the USD 20 HTTPS listing, which describes an
improved formula; an older HTTP USD 18 observation is not treated as a current
price. The four-scent set remains an unpriced lead because indexed offers conflict
(USD 65/value 72 versus USD 72/value 80). It is not interchangeable with the
three-scent set. Search results also contain regional currencies, which are not
merged into the US records. Buy and back-in-stock text coexist on rhode and r.e.m.
pages; availability is unresolved. PAPATUI's USD 8 is the brand's reference price,
not a verified direct checkout or retailer offer. Its tattoo stick is unpriced
because a generic homepage USD 10 lead did not establish the selected variant's
offer in the product extract.

e.l.f.'s acquisition completion release and September 2026 SEC filing establish
rhode's acquisition and subsidiary relationship. Its founder-role announcement
also identifies Michael D. Ratner and Lauren Ratner as cofounders. Coty's 2019
announcement and January 2020 completion release establish a historical 51%
transaction, not Kylie's current personal cap table. r.e.m.'s 2023 brand-issued
release identifies outside investors. Neither transaction totals nor company
financial figures are converted into personal proceeds or SKU margins.

Next research priorities: retailer reconciliation for PAPATUI, unresolved rhode
offers, exact package volumes, seller return policies, and original TikTok posts
with matching SKUs and commercial disclosures. Additional roster candidates
include James Charles, Logan Paul, Charli/Dixie D'Amelio and Addison Rae; their

## Original collection and financial boundaries

TikTok direct fetches returned no usable video content. Mikayla and Glamzilla
post evidence comes from indexed captions, not TikTok's AI-generated summaries.
Mrwhosetheboss's Sony evidence comes from Nutcake's copy of a caption and link.
Nikkie's MISSHA search results mixed unrelated snippets; a brand mention alone
does not justify attaching a specific BB cream or a price. No collected source
establishes a TikTok Shop product ID, attributed order or affiliate contract.

POV's one-time prices and conditional subscription prices are recorded separately.
Nimya prices come from the Netherlands EUR storefront and include tax; both
listings showed sold out. No currency conversion was performed. Nudestix's direct
product-page USD 49 conflicts with indexed sale offers of USD 19.60; both are
retained with provenance, and checkout reconciliation remains open. Sony's price
is for Platinum Silver, while the promotion's exact color is unverified. Current
L’Oréal reference prices are not asserted to be the historical posts' prices.

The shared `unknownEconomics` object applies to every record. Null means unknown,
never zero. The page cannot compute factual earnings from these observations.
Evidence needed to close those gaps includes creator-specific affiliate terms,
sponsorship/royalty contracts, attributed settled sales and refunds, net revenue,
COGS and variable costs. Personal profit additionally requires ownership,
distributions and creator expenses; company margin alone cannot establish it.

Calculators use only user-supplied assumptions. Merchant/brand results are per
unit. Gross profit subtracts COGS from net revenue; contribution also subtracts
variable costs. Fixed overhead, financing and income tax remain excluded.
Affiliate commission is gross commission before reversals, agency share, creator
expenses and tax. No assumed financial value is persisted, added to this dataset,
or used in the Onion Score. No scores are assigned to these real creators.
